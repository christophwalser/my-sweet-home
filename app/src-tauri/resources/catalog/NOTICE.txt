Default catalog of Sweet Home 3D
================================

This folder contains the default furniture and texture catalogs of Sweet Home 3D
(Furniture.jar, Textures.jar), shipped with My Sweet Home so it works without a
Sweet Home 3D installation. They are taken unchanged from a Sweet Home 3D
installation when the installer is built.
